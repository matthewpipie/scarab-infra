## Reuse distance, mlp_oracle_datacenter_defmark_ref

golden_small · 7 workloads, 20 simpoints · plain LRU (the reference run)

A reuse is a demand or instruction-prefetch lookup at the MLC of a line filled before. Its distance is the fills into the line's set since its previous reuse (since its first fill, for the first); a 16-way LRU set has evicted an untouched line after 16. Marked and unmarked are the line's class at the reuse (marked once any of its fills was marked). Unmarked instruction lines include those an instruction prefetch brought in with no demand waiting, and unmarked data lines those a store, writeback or data prefetch did. Columns give each class's share of its own reuses; the last two compare marked with unmarked lines in each bucket. Every workload weighs the same. For the control, see --miss-reuse.
